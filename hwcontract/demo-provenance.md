# Demo provenance

The NeoPixel demo downloads `ws2812b_neopixel24_24mhz.sr` from
[sigrok-dumps at f0d084a821370a4855a1305fc66439b6d3c54fce](https://github.com/sigrokproject/sigrok-dumps/tree/f0d084a821370a4855a1305fc66439b6d3c54fce/led/ws2812b_neopixel24).
It is a recorded 24-LED WS2812B ring, captured with a USBee SX at 24 MHz;
probe 1 is DATA_IN. The download is 111455 bytes and must match SHA256
`4d1558f7714f88a3a996bb90bb177e728976544efd8c6b03d38e1ba4ec644c26`.

The upstream [license statement](https://github.com/sigrokproject/sigrok-dumps/blob/f0d084a821370a4855a1305fc66439b6d3c54fce/README)
says dump files are released into the public domain by their authors unless
otherwise noted. The capture directory's README lists no exception. The capture
is downloaded separately, not redistributed inside the hwcontract wheel and
not relabeled as hwcontract's MIT code. It is cached under
`$XDG_CACHE_HOME/hwcontract`, or `~/.cache/hwcontract` by default. Use
`--cache-dir DIR`, `--capture PATH`, or `--offline` to control access.

SPI waveforms and serial boot logs are deterministic synthetic examples
generated by hwcontract's MIT-licensed code. SPI's B/E JSON roundtrip is an
artificial importer test, not output from a real sigrok SPI decoder. No
download, analyzer, or extra data asset is needed for those demos.
